# Gleamit Support

Gleamit is an oral hygiene tracker for iPhone and Android. It records brushing
sessions, streaks, per-tooth notes, dentist appointments and documents, brush
replacements and smile photos, all stored on your own device. There are no
accounts and no server.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you see my data?

No, and not because we promise not to look. Gleamit has no servers at all. There
is nowhere for your brushing history, notes, photos or documents to be sent, and
no account for them to be attached to, so there is nothing for us to look at,
lose, hand over or sell. Everything stays in the app on your phone.

The one exception is buying Gleamit Pro, which has to be checked with the App
Store or Google Play. That check confirms your purchase and nothing else: no
brushing data, no notes, no photos, no documents.

### I'm moving to a new phone. How do I keep my data?

In most cases your phone does it for you. Gleamit stores everything inside the
app's own storage, which your device's built-in backup already covers, so
restoring the new phone from that backup, or using the guided phone-to-phone
transfer, brings Gleamit back with it. Make sure the backup is switched on
before you wipe or hand over the old phone.

- **iPhone.** iCloud Backup carries everything: your history, teeth, reminders,
  dentist documents and smile photos. Restore the new iPhone from your iCloud
  backup and Gleamit comes back as it was.
- **Android.** Google's cloud backup carries your history, teeth, reminders and
  settings, but **not** your dentist documents and smile photos, so export those
  yourself. A direct phone-to-phone transfer (on recent Android versions) does
  carry them.

### Is that backup encrypted?

Yes. Your phone scrambles the backup before it leaves the device, and it goes
into your own Google or Apple account. It never passes through us, and we have
no way to see it.

Two things are worth knowing:

- **On Android**, the lock screen on your phone is what protects the backup. If
  your phone has a PIN, pattern or password, not even Google can read what is
  inside. If it has no lock screen, that protection does not apply, so it is
  worth setting one.
- **On iPhone**, Apple can open an iCloud backup if it has to, for example to
  help you recover your account. If you would rather nobody could, turn on
  **Advanced Data Protection** in your iCloud settings, and then only your own
  devices can read it.

The file you create with **Export full backup** is not scrambled, on purpose, so
that it stays yours to open. Keep it somewhere you trust, like any other
personal document.

### How do I make a copy of my data myself?

Use **Settings → Backup & export → Export full backup**. It writes one file
containing everything, history, teeth, reminders, documents and photos, that you
can keep on a cloud drive or computer and restore any time with **Import full
backup**.

This is worth doing in three cases: you are on Android and want your dentist
documents and smile photos to survive a lost or broken phone, since the export
is the only route for those; you are switching between Android and iPhone, which
no device backup crosses; or you simply want a copy that does not depend on
Google or Apple.

### I paid for Pro but the features are locked

Open the Pro screen and tap **Restore purchase**. Pro is a one-time purchase
tied to the store account that bought it, so make sure you are signed in to the
App Store or Google Play with that same account. If it still does not unlock,
email us with the store receipt and we will sort it out.

### How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. Everything Gleamit holds lives on your device, so removing the
app removes the data. Nothing is kept anywhere else, so there is no account to
close and no server-side copy to request.

### Why does Gleamit ask for camera and photo access?

Only to add pictures you choose to add: photos of your smile for the smile
timeline, and photos or scans of dentist documents such as X-rays and treatment
plans. The images stay on your device, and the app never uploads them.

### Is Gleamit a medical device? Will it tell me what to do?

No. Gleamit is a habit and record-keeping tool. It shows you what you entered
and attributes information to public health sources where relevant, but it does
not diagnose anything, assess anything you type in, or give medical advice. For
anything concerning your teeth or gums, see your dentist.

### Which languages does Gleamit support?

English, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese and Italian.
