## Description of Service

Gleamit is a personal oral hygiene tracking application. It lets you record and
manage brushing and floss sessions (manually or with the in-app timer), streaks,
history and trends derived from what you recorded, reminders (daily brushing,
brush replacement, professional cleaning and appointments), toothbrushes and
brush heads with replacement tracking, dentist information and appointments, a
tooth map (a condition and an optional note that you choose for each tooth,
with a history of your changes), dentist documents and smile photos you choose
to attach, and general educational content that is the same for every user and
is not driven by anything you enter.

You can generate a **dentist report** - a PDF built from what you recorded, with
a watermarked sample preview available before purchase - export and import a
**full backup archive** yourself, and turn on an **optional cloud backup** that
keeps a copy of your data in storage you control. An **optional one-time "Pro"
purchase** unlocks the dentist report, the full streak calendar history, all
trend charts, the complete per-tooth history, and unlimited dentist documents
and smile photos.

Gleamit works **entirely on your device**. There is **no Gleamit account and no
Gleamit backend**: your data is stored locally and is never sent to us. Our
[Privacy Policy](privacy_policy.md) describes what is stored and where backups
go.

## Medical Disclaimer

**Gleamit is not a medical device and does not give dental advice.** It is your
own record - it keeps track of what you log and shows it back to you. Always
consult your dental health professional when making decisions about your teeth.

Gleamit is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or
medical condition. In particular:

- **The tooth conditions and notes you record are your own notes, not
  findings.** You choose each tooth's condition yourself, including "Problem".
  The app stores that label and shows it back to you; it never examines,
  assesses, or interprets it.
- **The educational content is general information, not personalised advice.**
  It is the same static text for every user, it is not driven by anything you
  enter, and where it mentions what dental associations generally recommend it is
  reporting common general guidance rather than telling you what to do.
- **The dentist report is a self-reported summary, not a clinical record.** It
  renders what you entered - including the tooth conditions and notes you chose -
  and every page says so.

Nothing in the app takes the health information you enter and returns an
assessment of it: every feature is a tracking and record-keeping tool, provided
for convenience and habit support, and does not constitute medical advice.

Gleamit also offers an **optional cloud backup**, off until you turn it on,

yours and runs under Apple's or Google's terms, and the files are not encrypted
by Gleamit. Your device's own system backup does **not** include Gleamit data;

our [Privacy Policy](privacy_policy.md).

A backup can stop for reasons outside our control: your cloud storage is full,

backup back. The app surfaces such failures rather than failing quietly, but we

not responsible for the availability, contents, or timing of backups held in
