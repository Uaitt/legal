## Description of Service

Gleamit is a personal oral hygiene tracking application. It lets you record and
manage:

- **Brushing sessions**, recorded manually or with the in-app brushing timer.
- **Floss sessions.**
- **Streaks, history and trends** - counts and summaries derived from the
  sessions you recorded.
- **Reminders** - daily brushing reminders, plus maintenance reminders for brush
  replacement, professional cleaning and upcoming appointments.
- **Brush tracking** - toothbrushes and brush heads, with replacement tracking.
- **Dentist information** and your last cleaning date.
- **Dental appointments.**
- **A tooth map** - a condition and an optional note that you choose for each
  tooth, with a history of the changes you made.
- **Dentist documents** - files you choose to attach.
- **Smile photos** - photos you choose to add, with optional captions and dates.
- **Educational content** - general, static information about oral hygiene that
  is the same for every user and is not driven by anything you enter.
- **A dentist report** - a PDF you generate from what you recorded and share
  yourself, with a watermarked sample preview available before purchase.
- **A full backup archive** you can export and import yourself.
- **An optional cloud backup** that keeps a copy of your data in storage you
  control - Gleamit's own hidden area of your iCloud Drive on iOS, or the
  hidden app-scoped area of a Google account you connect on Android.
- **An optional one-time "Pro" purchase** that unlocks the dentist report, the
  full streak calendar history, all trend charts, the complete per-tooth history,
  and unlimited dentist documents and smile photos.

Gleamit works **entirely on your device**. There is **no Gleamit account and no
Gleamit backend**. Your data is stored locally on your device and is never sent
to us. If you turn on cloud backup, the copy goes into cloud storage of your
own, under your own Apple or Google account (see "Your Data and Backup"). On
Android that means connecting a Google account of yours: an optional sign-in
with Google, scoped to the hidden Gleamit-only area of your Drive and to nothing
else.

## Medical Disclaimer

**Gleamit is not a medical device and does not give dental advice.** It is your
own record - it keeps track of what you log and shows it back to you. Always
consult your dental health professional when making decisions about your teeth.

Gleamit is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or
medical condition. In particular:

- **The tooth conditions and notes you record are your own notes, not
  findings.** You choose each tooth's condition yourself, including "Problem".
  The app stores that label and shows it back to you; it never examines,
  assesses, or interprets it.
- **The educational content is general information, not personalised advice.**
  It is the same static text for every user, it is not driven by anything you
  enter, and where it mentions what dental associations generally recommend it is
  reporting common general guidance rather than telling you what to do.
- **The dentist report is a self-reported summary, not a clinical record.** It
  renders what you entered - including the tooth conditions and notes you chose -
  and every page says so.

Nothing in the app takes the health information you enter and returns an
assessment of it. Brushing timers, streaks, history, trends, reminders, dentist
information, the tooth map, appointments and the report are tracking and
record-keeping tools, provided for convenience and habit support, and do not
constitute medical advice.

## Your Data and Backup

Your app data is stored locally on your device. You are responsible for your
device and for keeping your own backups of any data that is important to you.

Gleamit provides an in-app export feature so you can create your own full
backup archive (including your tracking data as well as vault documents and
smile photos) and restore it later or on another device.

Files you generate to share, such as the dentist report PDF and the backup
archive, are created on your device and shared by you through your device's own
sharing options. They are not sent to us, and you decide who receives them.

Gleamit also offers an **optional cloud backup**, off until you turn it on. With
it on, a copy of your data - tracking records, dentist documents and smile
photos - is kept in storage you control: Gleamit's own hidden area of your
iCloud Drive on iOS, or the hidden app-scoped area of a Google account you
connect on Android. There is no Gleamit account in any of this. On iOS Gleamit
writes the files and Apple's own software syncs them under your Apple Account;
on Android Gleamit uploads them itself to your own Google Drive, using a
sign-in with Google scoped to that hidden area alone, and automatic uploads wait
for Wi-Fi unless you turn on "Back up using mobile data". Either way the storage
is yours and runs under that company's terms. The files are not encrypted by
Gleamit; they are ordinary files in storage you control, the same as an export
you make yourself.

Disconnecting Gleamit from your Google account in your Google Drive settings
deletes that hidden backup, because the area belongs to the app. Switching to
another Google account inside Gleamit, or disconnecting the account there,
leaves any existing copy untouched.

Your device's own system backup does **not** include Gleamit data: app backup is
switched off on Android, and on iOS Gleamit's working files are excluded from
the iCloud device backup - which is separate from the cloud backup described
above, the copy Gleamit puts in your storage on purpose. Restoring happens in
the app - on a new device you install
Gleamit and restore from your backup or from an exported archive; the operating
system does not bring your Gleamit data back on its own.

A backup can stop for reasons outside our control: your cloud storage is full,
Gleamit's access to your Google account was withdrawn, or iCloud Drive is off
for Gleamit. The app surfaces such failures rather than failing quietly, but we
do not guarantee that a backup exists, is current, or is complete, and we are
not responsible for the availability, contents, or timing of backups held in
your own storage. Keeping your own exported copy remains the safest option. Our
[Privacy Policy](privacy_policy.md) explains this in its "Cloud Backup" section.

If you uninstall the app or clear its storage, locally stored app data on that
device may be removed. For details, see our
[Privacy Policy](privacy_policy.md).
