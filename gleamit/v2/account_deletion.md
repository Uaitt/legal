# Gleamit Account and Data Deletion

**Last updated: May 6, 2026**

## Request Account Deletion

If you created a Gleamit account, you can request deletion of your account and
the data associated with that account.

## Option 1: Delete Your Account in the App

If you still have access to the Gleamit app:

1. Sign in to your Gleamit account.
2. Open the Profile tab.
3. Open the account screen.
4. Tap the delete account action.
5. Confirm the request by entering your password.

When the request succeeds, Gleamit deletes the backend account and the synced
backend data associated with that account immediately.

## Option 2: Request Deletion by Email

If you cannot access the app, send an email to:

**support@gleamit.app**

Please send the request from the email address associated with your Gleamit
account, or include that email address in the message.

Suggested subject line:

`Gleamit account deletion request`

## What to Include

To help us process the request, include:

- the email address associated with your Gleamit account
- a clear statement that you want your Gleamit account deleted

## What Will Be Deleted

When your Gleamit account is deleted, we delete the account and the synced
backend data associated with that account, including account-backed oral
hygiene tracking data such as:

- brushing sessions
- toothbrushes
- brush heads
- reminders
- dentist information
- appointments

## What May Remain

- If you still have the app installed on a device, some purely device-local
  preferences may remain on that device until you clear app storage or
  uninstall the app.
- We may retain limited data when required for legal, security, fraud
  prevention, or operational reasons, as described in our Privacy Policy.
